About Our Team

Welcome to the Burbank Stingrays

The Burbank Stingrays are a competitive swim team focused on helping swimmers develop technique, confidence, discipline, teamwork, and a lifelong appreciation for swimming.

Our team brings together swimmers, families, coaches, and the community through practices, competition, team events, and shared experiences.
