About Our Team

Welcome to the Burbank Stingrays

The Burbank Stingrays are a competitive swim team focused on helping swimmers improve their technique, confidence, discipline, teamwork, and competitive swimming experience.

Our team brings together swimmers, coaches, families, and the community through practices, meets, team events, and shared experiences.
